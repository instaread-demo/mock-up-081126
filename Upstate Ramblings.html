After years of cranking the oven in July and regretting it by dinner, I’ve learned to let the slow cooker handle the heat instead. These 19 Crockpot recipes cover backyard cookouts, easy weeknight dinners, and the lazy days when nobody wants to stand over a hot stove. I’ve included fall-apart ribs, pulled chicken, copycat takeout classics, hands-off soups, and sandwiches that build themselves while you’re at the pool.
