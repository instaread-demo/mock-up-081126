Hi, my name is Dr. Rebecca Bell. I’ve been practicing urogynecology for over 20 years. But these shocking statistics? They still send shivers down my spine.

78 million American women suffer from urinary incontinence.

But a recent report from the University of Michigan revealed something even worse. Two-thirds of these women don’t seek treatment for this issue.

Even though it strips away their quality of life and self-confidence.

Causing sudden urges to go, striking with uncontrollable pee leaks, and making them feel not as tight down there as they used to be.

That’s why I want to talk about the following three things:

  • What is the real root cause of urinary incontinence that most experts overlook
  • Why typical remedies like medications and Kegels don’t work
  • What are the top 5 supplements to end sudden urges and pee leaks for good

Also, I will explain which one (and why) of these 5 supplements my patients and I found to work the best. So, without further ado, let’s dive in.

So what is the real root cause of urinary incontinence that was discovered just recently?

You most likely already know about the gut microbiome. And you might have heard about the vaginal microbiome.

But what you probably aren’t aware of is the urinary microbiome.

It’s because scientists just recently found the whole bacterial ecosystem in the bladder that is crucial for its health. And this is already backed by reputable institutions like Harvard.

Now, the problem is that diet, stress, antibiotics, childbirth, menopause, periods, and even pads knock it out of balance.

And here’s exactly what happens after that:

  • Lactobacillus (the dominant good bacteria) vanishes from the bladder
  • Harmful microbes spread and multiply throughout the urinary tract
  • Overgrowth of bad bacteria causes the bladder to spasm and contract

That’s what causes sudden urges to go and uncontrollable pee leaks. Making women feel old well before their time.

But since this discovery is so recent, most doctors aren’t aware of it and suggest ineffective treatments

Now, before going any further, I want to stress one more important thing. Urinary incontinence can affect women at any age.

So the saying “it’s just a natural part of aging” is complete nonsense.

But I can understand why some medical experts say that. It’s because the treatments they suggest simply don’t work.

Or they only provide temporary relief at best.

Kegel exercises, for example, strengthen pelvic floor muscles. While medications forcefully reduce bladder spasms.

But none of them tackle urinary incontinence at its actual root.

Because it requires two steps: flushing out the bad bacteria from the urinary microbiome, and replenishing it with Lactobacillus.

So I put the five best-known bladder supplements to the test and ranked them by how completely they pull off those two steps.

Top 5 supplements to get rid of sudden urges to go and pee leaks for good

AZO Bladder Control with Go-Less

AZO is essentially Kegels in a capsule – pumpkin seed extract to tone the detrusor and sphincter muscles, and soy germ extract to support the pelvic floor.

That can help you hold on a little better in the short term. But it works only on the muscle. Not a single ingredient touches the bacteria driving urinary incontinence.

Verdict: Manages the symptoms only and leaves the actual cause untouched.

OxyFlo Bladder Control

OxyFlo takes a step toward the root. Built around cranberry, it helps stop harmful bacteria from sticking to the bladder wall – so it addresses the “flush out the bad” half.

But that’s where it stops. There’s nothing to replenish the protective Lactobacillus once the bad bacteria are cleared, so the microbiome never actually gets restored.

Verdict: Flushes out the bad bacteria, but doesn’t replenish the good.

MaxX Labs Advanced Bladder Control

MaxX Labs casts a wider net – cranberry and hibiscus to reduce pathogen adhesion, pumpkin seed for a little muscle support, plus green tea, vitamin D3, and apple cider vinegar.

That muscle support may help manage leaks a bit, but it still doesn’t tackle the real root cause. Broader than OxyFlo, but still no ingredient that rebuilds the protective Lactobacillus.

Verdict: A broader flush-and-tone blend, but still no Lactobacillus replenishment.

FemiClear Daily Bladder Strength + Urinary Tract Flush

This is actually a two-supplement system. FemiClear pairs caper bark, lindera root, and horsetail with cranberry and hyaluronic acid to support the bladder wall and calm urgency.

Then adds D-mannose and a plant antimicrobial blend (bearberry, birch leaf, berberine, forskolin) to flush harmful bacteria from the urinary tract.

If anything here was going to challenge my #1 pick, it’s this. But you’re buying two supplements instead of one, so  its monthly price is naturally higher than a normal one-bottle supply.

Verdict: Excellent at clearing the harmful bacteria, but does very little to replenish the good.

Second – and this is unique in this lineup – it adds two Bifidobacterium strains (B. breve and B. longum) that actively target and clear out the harmful bacteria.

Third, it feeds them the right fuel – prebiotic fructooligosaccharides, which nourish both the Lactobacillus and the Bifidobacterium so they survive and multiply.

Fourth, it includes cranberry whole fruit powder – it doesn’t just guard against UTIs, but physically stops bad bacteria from sticking to the bladder walls.

Fifth, it’s the only one here with Tributyrin, a compound that soothes inflammation and helps reverse the damage the bad bacteria left behind.

And sixth, it’s delivered in delayed-release capsules, so all these ingredients actually survive stomach acid and reach the bladder, where they’re needed.
