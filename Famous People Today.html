She started at Fox News as a contributor in 2017 and was named a co-host on The Five in early 2022.

There are rumors about Jessica Tarlov leaving The Five or being fired by Fox.

The first rumors sparked on X in February 2024.

“Jessica Tarlov has been released by FOX,” user @DougWahl1 calimed on X (formerly Twitter) states. “She apparently was too successful at making her left leaning points, and showing up Jesse Walters.”

The post received 1 million views on X in less than 24 hours.

After @DougWahl1’s post on X went viral, Irena Briganti, Senior Executive Vice President for Corporate Communications at Fox News Media, stated, “This is completely false.”

In case you didn’t know, Jesse Watters is also a TV host who works for Fox.

Over the years, Tarlov and Watters have engaged in several on-air disagreements.

For more on their dynamic, you may find this video insightful.

So, did Jessica Tarlov get fired from Fox’s The Five?

Jessica Tarlov attends Fox News’ Super Tuesday 2024 primary election coverage at Fox News Channel Studios on March 05, 2024 in New York City – @Getty

It certainly sounds like a juicy rumor. Small problem: It’s not true. Jessica Tarlov has not been fired, despite a post on X on Feb. 8. However, in the past, she was absent from The Five for various reasons.

For instance, Tarlov took maternity leave from Fox News’ The Five following the birth of her second daughter, Teddy Tarlov McKenna, in April 2024.

Tarlov announced her temporary departure on social media, stating, “Will be on maternity leave for [the] next few months, so to the haters and the losers, enjoy the break! I know I will with my girls.”

Jessica Tarlov attends Variety Power Of Women New York 2024 – @Getty

Note – her first daughter, Cleo, was born in December 2021.

The father of her daughters is hedge fund executive Brian McKenna.

Tarlov and Brian McKenna wed in 2021.

She returned to The Five in July 2024.

“So excited to be back from maternity leave! And what a week,” she wrote on X on July 15, 2024. “Teddy and Cleo are in great hands and see you on the Five from the RNC.”

Jessica has been open about the challenges of balancing her career and motherhood.
