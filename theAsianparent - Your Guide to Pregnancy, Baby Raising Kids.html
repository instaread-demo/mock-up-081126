6 Best Organic Food Stores in Singapore (2026 Updated List)

We've done the research for you and compiled a list of 6 organic food stores in Singapore.

Here in Singapore, we are lucky to be surrounded by an abundance of fresh food from all around the world, including organic food stores.

However, “fresh” doesn’t necessarily mean “pesticide-free” or “hormone-free.” If you are considering giving your family pure produce that doesn’t contain nasty artificial additives, then you really have just one option: to buy organic. But where can you find organic food stores?

We’ve done the research for you and compiled a list of 6 organic food stores in Singapore.

1. Organic Food Singapore Stores: Brown Rice Paradise

Brown Rice Paradise was started in 1994 and is one of Singapore’s most established organic food stores.

It offers a huge range of organic items, including gluten-free, wheat-free and dairy-free products; low-sugar and low-sodium products; healthy snacks and drinks; organic baby food and baby care products; vitamins; and household and personal care products.

Locations: Blk 352 Clementi Shopping Centre Clementi Avenue 2, #01-105 Singapore 120352

Phone no.: +65 8930 8081

Email: contact@brownriceparadise.com

Opening hours: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Mon – Sat) Closed on Sunday

Website: brownriceparadise.com

2. The Organic Grocer

The Organic Grocer has been in operation since 2010, bringing together the best organic brands and produce from Singapore and around the world under one roof. It has an impressive range of organic items, ranging from vegetables, fruits and bakery items to rice, pasta, grains and noodles…and many more.

What’s more, The Organic Grocer offers a great delivery service for free that will bring your order to you within 48 hours of receiving the items from Australia or the USA. This way your order gets delivered to you fresh.

Location: 17 Mohamed Sultan Rd Singapore 238966

Phone no.: (+65) 6469 7743

Calling hours: Open daily from 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM

Website: https://theorganicgrocer.com/index.html

3. Organic Food Singapore Stores: SuperNature

Established in 2001, SuperNature started as a little store. Now it is one of Singapore’s leading organic and natural products retailers.

SuperNature has a selection of fresh produce, meats, groceries, baby and children’s needs, beauty and wellness and household products from the most ethical organic producers around the world. It also supplies many of Singapore’s organic retailers and restaurants and offers free delivery for online purchases over $70.

Locations: 26 Dempsey Rd, #01-01, Singapore 249686

Phone no:  +65 6854 7399

Calling hours: open daily from 8.00 AM to 8.00 PM

Website: https://www.eshop.supernature.com.sg/index.php

4. Organic Food Singapore Stores: Nature’s Superfoods

Nature’s Superfoods is an organic food brand created by Actspand Pte Ltd. It prides itself on its high quality organic food, while supplying to distributors, wholesalers and manufacturers.

Brought to consumers at the best possible price and value, the range of great products available at Nature’s Superfoods include black and white chia seeds, organic cacao powder, organic extra virgin coconut oil, red quinoa seeds and many more.

Location: 12 Arumugam Road, LTC Building B #03-09, Singapore 409958.

Phone no.: +65 6841 5476 / 6841 5304

Opening hours: Monday to Friday, 8:45AM – 5:30PM (Excluding Public Holidays)

5. The Fishwives

Rebecca Forwood is the orginal Fishwife. She started the business in Hong Kong in 1992 and set up in Singapore in 2002.

Rebecca’s passion has always been about healthy unadulterated fresh food. After a lot of ongoing research, she has found products that tick all the boxes when it comes to healthy and “clean” food.

The Fishwives product list encompasses seafood and meat, and offers fresh organic produce such as NZ King Salmon, line caught snapper, pasture fed Angus Beef and pasture fed lamb all from New Zealand. The Fishwives also offer fresh Hormone and Antibiotic Free French Chicken, which is raised in a free-range environment in Malaysia and delivered daily to Singapore.

All products imported by The Fishwives are free of hormones, antibiotics, mercury, steroid, growth enhancers and genetic modifications. You can also order the products online.

Location: 501 Bukit Timah Rd, #01-05B GROUND LEVEL, Singapore 259764 

Phone no.: +65 8809 0938

Email: info@thefishwives.com

Opening hours: Monday, Tuesday & Friday: 10am – 4pm, Wednesday & Thursday: Closed, Saturday & Sunday: 10am – 3pm

Website: https://www.thefishwives.com

6. Yes Natural

Yes Natural has been Singapore’s go-to destination for organic groceries, natural health products, and wholesome vegetarian living since 2006. With over 2,500 carefully selected products available online and in-store, they make it easy to find everything you need for a healthier, more conscious lifestyle.

They carry a comprehensive range of organic groceries, plant-based foods, vegan snacks, natural health supplements, and traditional Chinese herbs — all rigorously selected to be free from artificial additives, preservatives, and GMO ingredients. Whether you’re stocking your pantry, supporting your wellness routine, or exploring the benefits of Chinese herbal products, Yes Natural has you covered.

Their products are sourced from trusted producers worldwide, with a focus on clean labels, minimal processing, and genuine nutritional value. From fresh organic vegetables to eco-friendly household essentials, every product on their shelves meets our strict standards for quality and integrity.

Location: 53 Lor 27 Geylang Singapore 388181

Phone no: +65 6846 1580

Opening hours: Mon – Sun: 10:30am – 8:30pm, Public Holidays: ​Open as usual

Website: https://yesnatural.sg/
