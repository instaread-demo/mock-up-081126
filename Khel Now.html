Fans rally in support of Jamshedpur FC amid ISL exit

With uncertainty surrounding Jamshedpur FC‘s future, Jamshedpur’s passionate supporters from all age groups came together in a powerful show of togetherness on 3rd August, Monday at the Armory ground, Northern town, Jamshedpur.

The passionate fans gathered in numbers to voice their opposition of the club’s exit from Indian football, conveying the message to the stakeholders to preserve one of Indian football’s most important football institutions. This peaceful demonstration clearly shows the love for the club that the city has and how deeply rooted it is to Jamshedpur’s lifestyle.

Indian football fans from every corner of the country consisting of various fan groups have come in support to save Jamshedpur FC and preserve a historic part of Indian football.

Multiple former Jamshedpur FC players and Indian footballers have also come in support of the club and urged the higher ups to reconsider their decision for the sake of Indian football.

More than a club for the fans

Today the people of Jamshedpur have shown that this club represents more than just 90 minutes of football on the pitch. It represents the city’s identity, pride and togetherness. As uncertainty surrounds the club’s future fans turned up in number to appeal to the higher ups of TATA to reconsider their decision and allow Jamshedpur FC to compete in ISL moving forward. The message was clear: the city is not ready to lose a football team that became an integral part of their lives.

The fans carried banners like “Jam ke Khelo Jamshedpur”, “Dil Dil Jamshedpur” and “Save Jamshedpur FC” passionately expressing their love for the club.

A Jamshedpur FC fan shared an emotional message during the rally.

The fan said, “When someone’s home is taken away, they are left homeless. In the same way, if Jamshedpur FC is shut down, we too will be left homeless.”

This clearly shows us that fans are always an integral part of any sports team. When there is a situation of trouble, the fans are the ones who always come together to support the team in any situation.

Indian football community comes in support of Jamshedpur FC

The Indian football community have shown immense maturity as they have gathered to support the club and its identity. From players, fans to club owner every stakeholder of Indian football wants Jamshedpur FC back and have urged the TATA higher ups to reconsider their decision for the sake of Indian football and save this historic club.

Players like Nikhil Poojary, Rahul Bheke, Sandesh Jhingan, Sunil Chhetri, Tiri, Javi Hernandez and many more have raised their voice regarding this matter. Bengaluru FC owner Mr. Parth Jindal has expressed his disappointment and concern over Jamshedpur’s exit.

The players of Jamshedpur FC have also shared a video online where they have urged the higher ups to save this club and reconsider their decision.

In these difficult times, this kind of support clearly shows just how deeply Indian football is connected to the fans. It proves that football is more than just a sport, it is an emotion that unites people and generations together.

Bhaichung Bhutia makes a bold statement on India's friendly against Brazil on 3rd October

By Rohit Mistri
A sports journalist passionate about on-ground reporting and football storytelling.
Published at :August 3, 2026 at 8:41 AM
Modified at :August 5, 2026 at 10:01 AM

India will face the five-time World Cup winners in Kolkata

A history in making for Indian Football after the friendly game against Brazil has been announced by both the All Indian Football Federation (AIFF) and the Brazilian Football Federation on 3rd October 2026.

But as India football legend Bhaichung Bhutia raised concerns regarding this game when the Indian football team look to give their best against the Samba side, as a team like India might face big troubles in the match.

Bhaichung Bhutia was quoted by Sportstar as saying “It’s like a struggling family bringing SRK to dance at their son’s wedding,”

And before a big game like this, it might pull big controversies.

The five-time World Cup champions are set to travel to Australia first to play a game against the Matildas, and then, the next stop, they will face the Blue Tigers at the Salt Lake Stadium.

This match will not only give fans in India a dream to watch their favourite footballing superstars but also give Indian football a bit more recognition and global exposure. 

Bhaichung Bhutia raises concerns over the Brazil vs India clash despite excitement

Indian football legend Bhaichung Bhutia expressed major concerns over the clash, which was announced earlier this week, as the Brazilian national side is set to play a friendly game against the Indian football team in Kolkata.

This news spread out very fast as many people in India were fond of the Brazilian team as they followed the Joga Bonito style in the FIFA World Cup, Copa America or any other international competitions.

But ahead of the game, Bhaichung Bhutia stated that big as it might be, controversial as he mentioned Indian football is like a family that doesn’t have food right now, and they are trying to bring Bollywood actor Shah Rukh Khan to dance at their family wedding.

The former India skipper stressed the urge to reconstruct the current state of Indian football, and it should be the top priority along with improving grassroots football.

“I’m not saying they should not come, but at this point, given the condition of Indian football, what are we looking to achieve out of this match? Jamshedpur FC closed (opted out of ISL), the teams are not going to the Asian games, and our FIFA (men’s) ranking is now almost 140,”

“The date, too, I think, should be changed because you may not have the first team playing because it would be in the FIFA ASEAN Cup. Are they going to play with a second team, that too against Brazil?”

“At this moment, I think the focus should be on getting the restructuring right. It’s like a family that does not have proper food – and it’s like a struggling family bringing SRK to dance at their son’s wedding.” Bhaichung Bhutia added.

How big will this India vs Brazil clash be?

The friendly game against Brazil is one of the biggest international football matches ever to be hosted in India, and it will be against the Blue Tigers on 3rd October 2026 in Kolkata as per AIFF has revealed earlier.

The five-time World Cup Champions are set to face one of the most football loving Nations India, which will mark a great historic feat for the current scenario of Indian football.

It will also mark the first time the Brazilian senior men’s national team play an official international friendly match on Indian soil.

The super clash between them is expected to fill the Salt Lake Stadium with thousands of supporters who have supported the Samba side in the World Cup or any international competitions, and all are hoping that their own National side could play a very good game against Carlo Ancelotti’s Brazil, which will bring excitement and global exposure to football.

It is also a very good chance for the players to test themselves against one of the strongest National teams in the world, and from this, the upcoming game against Brazil will not only be a remarkable match for the Indian Football community, but also it builds global attention.

Where will the India vs Brazil match be played?

India will face Brazil in an international friendly on October 3, 2026. The match will be played at the Salt Lake Stadium (Vivekananda Yuba Bharati Krirangan) in Kolkata.

What did Bhaichung Bhutia say about the match?

Bhutia compared the situation to “a struggling family bringing Shah Rukh Khan to dance at their son’s wedding,” suggesting that Indian football should prioritise fixing its problems before hosting glamour matches.

Why has Bhaichung Bhutia criticised the fixture?

Bhaichung Bhutia believes Indian football should first focus on rebuilding its structure. He pointed to Jamshedpur FC’s withdrawal from the ISL, India’s low FIFA ranking, and other issues, saying these should be addressed before organising high-profile friendlies.
