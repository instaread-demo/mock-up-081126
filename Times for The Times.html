Times Quick Cryptic 3370 by Pipsqueak – Spooner’s style moan

Hi all.  It’s been a while since my last Pipsqueak, and a welcome return.  No hold-ups for me (nor suspenders, haha, ok I won’t give up the day job).  When reading back to try to identify highlights I was struck by how skilfully the definitions are incorporated into lovely smooth surfaces.  For my favourite clue, I will go with the funny business in 21a.  Thanks Pipsqueak!

(I should mention that the “style moan” is nothing to do with the puzzle, just self-indulgently noting a personal blogging milestone.)

Definitions are underlined in the clues below.  In the explanations, I generally italicise indicators unless it seems clearer not to.  Where the removed part is specified, [deletions] are in square brackets, and I’ve capitalised and emboldened letters which appear in the ANSWER.  I sometimes omit link words and juxtaposition indicators if it doesn’t feel necessary to explain them.  If you have any questions, please ask in the comments section.

Across
1a Trouble following idiot’s attack (6)
ASSAIL — AIL (trouble) following ASS (idiot)
4a Get what shipwrecked sea dog longs for? (4)
LAND — A double definition
9a Spotted African runner — adulterer they say (7)
CHEETAH — Sounds like (… they say) CHEATER (adulterer)
10a Brash American hiding embarrassment (5)
SHAME — BraSH AMErican is hiding the answer
11a Star female put down oil producer (9)
SUNFLOWER — SUN (star) + F (female) + LOWER (put down)
12a Elderly husband avoiding embrace (3)
OLD — H (husband) omitted from (avoiding) [h]OLD (embrace)
13a Briefly express view after doctor’s visit (4,2)
DROP IN — Without the last letter (briefly) OPINe (express view) after DR (doctor)
15a Heartlessly examine very small creature (6)
INSECT — INSpECT (examine) missing its central letter (heartlessly …)
17a Fate of item for sale at Sotheby’s (3)
LOT — Two definitions
18a Emir’s text disturbed fanatic (9)
EXTREMIST — An anagram of (… disturbed) EMIR’S TEXT
21a Funny business with the Speaker’s assistant? (5)
COMIC — CO (business) + MIC (the speaker’s assistant?)
22a Men upset with girl, mischievous little thing (7)
GREMLIN — An anagram (upset) of MEN with GIRL
23a Furious about knight’s curse (4)
DAMN — MAD (furious) backwards (about) + N (knight, chess notation)
24a Some resist a tickle still (6)
STATIC — Some resiST A TICkle
Down
1d American clubs repeatedly exploited defendant (7)
ACCUSED — A (American) + C C (clubs repeatedly – two copies of the abbreviation used in card games) + USED (exploited)
2d Southern seabird’s back (5)
STERN — S (southern) + TERN (seabird)
3d Fancy telling niece what CIA is after! (12)
INTELLIGENCE — An anagram of (fancy) TELLING NIECE
5d A bishop by himself eating this? (7)
ABALONE — A + B (bishop) + ALONE (by himself)
6d Fear old man is catching on (5)
DREAD — DAD (old man) goes around (is catching) RE (on, regarding)
7d Farm animal eating hot dog (4)
CHOW — COW (farm animal) taking in (eating) H (hot)
8d Alienation of curious people in Spielberg film (12)
ESTRANGEMENT — STRANGE MEN (curious people) in E.T. (Spielberg film – the commonly used shortened version of the full title)
14d Choose one parent as model (7)
OPTIMUM — OPT (choose) + I (one) + MUM (parent)
16d Picture intact, I suspect (7)
TITANIC — INTACT, I anagrammed (suspect)
17d Clear line head of UK police supports (5)
LUCID — L (line) and the first letter of (head of) UK, which CID (police) goes beneath (supports)
19d Roman attire, article acquired from the south (4)
TOGA — In reverse (from the south, in a down entry) A (article) and GOT (acquired)
20d Popular place to rent in bay (5)
INLET — IN (popular) + LET (place to rent)
