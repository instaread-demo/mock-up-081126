    AAA Lucha Libre Worldwide results (8/8): Vetter’s review of Flammer vs. Lady Shani vs. La Catalina for the AAA Reina de Reinas Title, El Fiscal vs. Psycho Clown vs. Cruz Del Toro in a qualifying match

    August 9, 2026

    CategoriesAAA TV Reviews MUST-READ LIST NEWS TICKER TV REVIEWS

    By Chris Vetter, ProWrestling.net Contributor (@chrisvetter73)

    Listen to audio version of this article
    Sponsored by
    0:00 / 6:35
    Instaread

    Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide (Episode 30)
    Taped July 25, 2026, in Aguascalientes, Mexico, at Arena San Marcos
    Streamed August 8, 2026, on Fox in Latin America and on the WWE YouTube Channel

    Corey Graves, Rey Mysterio, and JBL were on commentary. The show’s title means “Summer of Scandal.”

    * Wrestlers were shown arriving at the venue.

    1. Cruz Del Toro vs. Psycho Clown vs. Fiscal in a three-way qualifier for the four-way match for the vacant AAA Latin American Championship. Cruz was kicked to the floor, so Clown and Fiscal traded lucha reversals. Fiscal hit a powerbomb on Cruz at 1:30. Clown hit a pop-up punch on Fiscal. Cruz hit a huracanrana on Clown. Cruz hit a top-rope twisting dive onto both opponents at 3:00. In the ring, he hit a dropkick, sending Clown to the floor. Fiscal hit a suplex on Clown at 4:30. On the apron, Clown hit an Air Raid Crash on Cruz. He clotheslined Fiscal to the floor.

    A tall man in a mask in the front row stood up and attacked Clown! (If this was AEW, I would think it was Luchasaurus!) The commentators had no idea who this guy is! Meanwhile, Cruz and Fiscal kept fighting in the ring. Those two fought on the top rope, and Fiscal threw him to the mat. Fiscal hit a top-rope frog splash for the pin! An unexpected winner. We saw a replay of this tall, masked man, wearing bib overalls, attacking Psycho Clown.

    El Fiscal defeated Cruz Del Toro and Psycho Clown at 7:46 to advance to the four-way match for the vacant AAA Latin American Championship.

    * Rey Mysterio talked about Raw coming to Mexico, and also about the eight-man tournament, with the winner facing Roman Reigns.

    2. Noisy Boy vs. Jack Cartwheel. Graves noted this is just Jack’s second match back since he dislocated his elbow in Las Vegas in April. (He is still wearing a long sleeve on his left arm.) Quick lucha reversals and Jack knocked him to the floor. The masked Noisy Boy hit a springboard stunner for a nearfall. Noisy Boy hit a springboard-back-splash at 2:00. Jack got up and hit some chops. They traded forearm strikes. Jack hit his impressive Crucifix Driver for a nearfall. JBL made many cartwheel-related puns.

    Noisy Boy hit a cool Frankensteiner for a nearfall at 3:30. “At this point, these guys are making stuff up!” Graves said. Jack hit his slingshot elbow drop, then the Sasuke Special dive to the floor. In the ring, Jack hit a top-rope Phoenix Splash for the pin. I’ve been watching Jack for several years now, but many WWE fans are going to be dazzled by him for the very first time.

    Jack Cartwheel defeated Noisy Boy at 4:30.

    * Karmen Petrovic seductively strolled down the ring, and the crowd popped — the fans know what this means! The four male members of Perros Del Mal jumped in the ring, and they all beat up Cartwheel. Karmen finished it off with her Petrifyer spin kick to Jack’s head. Daga spoke on the mic, and Rey provided translation. Daga talked about how they keep making the rules, and they learned from the best. “Once a dog, always a dog.”

    * Damian 666 came to the ring, wearing his face paint and carrying a kendo stick. (He made a brief appearance on TV in the last week or two.) He got into the ring and hugged each of the Perros Del Mal members. Daga gave Damian a shirt, and he put it on. Damian pulled a chain out of his pocket and put it around his neck. He spoke in Spanish, asking, “Where you at, dogs?”

    * Andrea (girlfriend of El Grande Americano) interviewed Dominik backstage as they were seated in chairs across from each other. Dominik tried to explain why he walked out on El Grande Americano in a recent tag match. He vowed he would beat him in the center of the ring. (Because this segment was pre-taped, there were subtitles.) Andrea asked him about teaming up with Dorian Roldan. Dominik talked about how his imbecile father became general manager. He vowed that wherever he goes, the seven-footer Omos will be there with him. Liv Morgan entered the room and glared at Andrea. Liv and Dominik stormed out of the interview; Liv didn’t say a word.

    * A video clip aired setting up the main event.

    3. Flammer vs. Lady Shani vs. La Catalina in a three-way for the Reina de Reinas Title. During the ring introductions, Rey said he needed to leave to “take care of some GM stuff.” Interesting. Flammer immediately tried chops on each opponent. Shani and Catalina hit stereo buttbumps on Flammer. Those two then traded reversals while Flammer was on the floor. Shani dove to the floor onto Flammer. Catalina dove off the top turnbuckle onto both on the floor at 1:30. In the ring, Shani chopped Catalina, then she dropped Flammer face-first into the middle turnbuckle. She hit Facewash kicks on each opponent.

    Flammer hit a basement dropkick at 3:30. She stomped on Shani’s stomach. Catalina hit a top-rope missile dropkick on both women at 5:00. She bodyslammed Shani, then hit a Gory Bomb, dropping Flammer onto Shani for a nearfall! Catalina spun Shani on the mat. Flammer kicked her. Shani now spun Flammer on the mat and got a nearfall at 6:30. Flammer hit a sit-out powerbomb on Shani. Catalina hit a frog splash onto Shani for a nearfall, but Flammer made the save. On the floor, Catalina hit a spear on Flammer at 8:00! In the ring, Shani and Catalina traded rollups. Catalina hit a buttbump and a Shining Wizard on Shani. However, Flammer jumped back into the ring, threw Catalina to the floor, and stole the pin on Shani! Good action.

    Lady Flammer defeated Lady Shani and La Catalina in a three-way to retain the Reina De Reinas Title at 9:09.

    Final Thoughts: An enjoyable episode. I’m sure someone who knows the Mexican wrestling scene will know who the tall masked guy was who attacked Psycho Clown, but I have no idea who he is. A really good showing for Cartwheel, who has been so good for GCW and more recently, in Evolve. The women’s three-way was fun. No real complaints, but it’s unclear why Rey had to leave during the main event — I assumed he would appear on TV before the show concluded, but that wasn’t the case.
