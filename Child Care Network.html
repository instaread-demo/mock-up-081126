5 Reasons to Enroll Your Child in an After School Program

Afterschool care provides a safe, structured, and enriching environment where children can learn, grow, and build independence while giving families peace of mind.

July 20, 2026

As your child gets older, you may be wondering if afterschool care makes sense for your family. You might think, “my child is old enough to stay home alone, why would I enroll them in an afterschool program?” An afterschool program is not just a place for your child to hang out; it is a time for them to learn, play, socialize, and grow in a safe space while you work. As you think over your options for the upcoming school year, here are a few benefits for you to keep in mind.

1. Afterschool care provides safety and supervision

Research shows that the hours after school are when most older children participate in unsafe risk behaviors, like fighting, dangerous physical actions including climbing or jumping from unsafe places, or looking at inappropriate
websites on the tablet. When you enroll your child in an afterschool program, you can trust they’re engaged in fun, age-appropriate activities within a safe, adultsupervised environment.

2. Afterschool care helps build a consistent and predictable routine

Children thrive on routines. Knowing what to expect each afternoon helps them feel confident, secure, and ready to participate. From familiar teachers to consistent schedules and trusted friends, afterschool care creates a routine that helps children transition smoothly from the school day into an enjoyable afternoon.

3. Afterschool care supports learning and academic growth

Learning does not have to end when school lets out. Afterschool programs often include games, activities, and projects that will help your child practice concepts they learned in school in a fun way. Our afterschool curriculum, Young Achiever’s Club, focuses on turning academic concepts into interactive games to get children excited to learn! Many of our afterschool programs include dedicated time for homework, giving children a chance to get a head start before heading home.

4. Afterschool care builds independence and self-help skills

Afterschool care can help promote personal growth skills that last well into adulthood. Children are given the opportunity to develop skills that will help with time management, self-motivation, and responsibility. Some of these opportunities include:

  • Serving themselves snack
  • Choosing their activities for the afternoon
  • Planning out their time in the classroom

5. Afterschool care sparks imagination and creativity

A new environment and new friends opens up all kinds of opportunities for creativity. Kids are encouraged to grow their social skills by working and playing with peers with different thoughts and abilities, which will also help them think more creatively! In our afterschool curriculum, Young Achiever’s Club, children are encouraged to play, explore, and create with their friends through independent play, or teacher-led projects.

In the end, choosing afterschool care is about giving your child more than just a place to spend the afternoon—it’s about giving them opportunities to thrive. A high-quality program provides the safety, structure, and support children need
while also encouraging their independence, creativity, and love of learning. As your family prepares for the school year ahead, consider how an afterschool program can enrich your child’s daily experience and bring you peace of mind. It’s not simply about filling time after school—it’s about helping your child grow into a confident, capable, and well-rounded individual.
