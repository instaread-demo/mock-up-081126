Could The Marshall Mathers LP3 Arrive in 2026?

By ePro Team / August 3, 2026

Instaread

Eminem has been silent for a year now. Fans have filled that silence with theories, guesses, dates, titles and calculations. Are there any leads?

Eminem dropped The Death of Slim Shady (Coup de Grâce) on July 12, 2024. More than two years later, he has offered very little new material.

His last newly recorded verse appeared on JID’s “Animals (Pt. 1)” in July 2025. However, the Stans project brought an old song from the vault that August. “Everybody’s Looking at Me” had remained unreleased since the early 2000s before finally appearing on the documentary soundtrack.

Since then, nothing. For a year, we have been living without a new Eminem song or feature. So, no wonder that fans come up with theories that give them hope, and those theories have caught so much attention.

The magic of numbers

The general theory relies heavily on numbers and dates. It starts with a neat gap. The Marshall Mathers LP arrived in 2000. Then, The Marshall Mathers LP 2 followed in 2013.

Therefore, 2026 would give the series three release years placed 13 years apart. However, this works only by counting calendar years, rounding the chronological distance down generously. The first two albums were actually released more than 13 years apart: MMLP on May 23, 2000 and MMLP2 on November 5, 2013.

Still, the next record would be Eminem’s 13th studio album. That count includes his 1996 debut, Infinite.

Moreover, Eminem’s full name is Marshall Bruce Mathers III. A third album bearing his real name would make a pleasing link, fans think.

Yet none of those facts points to an actual plan. They form a smart pattern, but patterns are not evidence. Still, humans look for patterns when they try to make sense of the world, especially when they need signs of hope.

Looking for patterns

Following the same logic, fans look into the album order and find more suggestive clues. Four studio records separate the first two Marshall Mathers LP albums: The Eminem Show, Encore, Relapse and Recovery. Likewise, four albums have followed MMLP2: Revival, Kamikaze, Music to Be Murdered By and The Death of Slim Shady. Therefore, MMLP3 would follow the same rhythm, closing the gap with a familiar bookend.

The decade theory also fits. MMLP was Eminem’s first studio album of the 2000s. Meanwhile, MMLP2 was his second of the 2010s. An MMLP3 would be his third studio album of the 2020s. It would also place one title from the series in each decade.

Again, though, several points describe the same coincidence. They are not separate clues, and more importantly, there is nothing to suggest that this rhythm was deliberate, or that Eminem would feel any need to keep following it.

Similarly, both Curtain Call collections would sit between the three MMLP albums. The first came in 2005, while Curtain Call 2 followed in 2022.

That symmetry looks good on a timeline. However, it says nothing about work inside the studio or an artistic concept.

A creative argument

The strongest part of the theory has little to do with numbers, but rooted in storytelling. The Death of Slim Shady staged a battle between Eminem and his famous alter ego. Therefore, a return to Marshall Mathers could form a logical next chapter. Shady has received his funeral. Marshall must now decide what remains.

Still, Eminem may not want another sequel. MMLP2 already revisited his old home, public image and uneasy bond with fame. A third record would need its own reason to exist.

Full-circle moment

Finally, fans have suggested November 13, 2026, as a possible release date. It falls on a Friday, the usual day for major albums. It also comes one day after the 30th anniversary of Infinite. Eminem’s debut was released on November 12, 1996.

That would be excellent marketing. However, Eminem fans know by now that marketing concerns do not drive his release schedule. A new album comes when Marshall feels ready and has something he wants to say, and never to mark an anniversary.

Understandably, the fans are longing for new music. When Paul Rosenberg hinted in November 2025 that Eminem busy meeting deadlines for mysterious projects and stated that “The music currently in production approaches completion”, many believed a new album might be only months away.

And maybe it is. Yet there is no official announcement to sustain hope or to give any credibility to those theories.

Eminem’s creative process is not ruled by the primitive magic of numbers. Yet the theory speaks to a real hunger. After a quiet year, fans simply want to hear where Marshall goes next.
