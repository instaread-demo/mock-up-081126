Joel Harrison: Live at Big Ears-In A Silent Way Reimagined

by George W. Harris • August 1, 2026 • 0 Comments
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 0:55
Instaread

In celebration of Miles Davis’ 100 birthday, guitarist Joel Harrison salutes The Prince of Darkness by revisiting the seminal fusion album, 1969’s In A Silent Way at the Big Ears Festival in Knoxville, Tennessee. He’s on stage with Brandon Seabrook-Silas the Soundmaker/g, Sam Sidursky/ss, Micah Thomas/key, Jerome Harris/b, Chad Taylor/b and Tim Keiper/perc for extrapolation of the original tunes, plus a self penned addendum. The 24 minute “Shhh/Shalome” includes a surfeit of guitar effects and noodles, slowly evolving into a spacey groove led by Harris bas solo, with the guitars and saxes jabbing like a Rocky Balboa, all propulsed by Taylor’s extroverted sticks into a coalesce and release of tension. The 37 minute “In A Silent Way/What About Time” has a bucketload of Hendrixy experiences, chaotic and distorted before going  into a 6 if it was a 9, slowly undulating into a black light poster room of meditative grooviness. Wide wale chords.
