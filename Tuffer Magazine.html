How to Choose the Right Home Care Agency in McLean: 7-Point Checklist

  1. Verify Virginia licensure. Confirm the agency holds a valid Home Care Organization license with the Virginia Department of Health.
  2. Ask about caregiver screening. National background checks, drug screening, CNA certification, and ongoing training should be standard.
  3. Confirm employment model. Agencies that employ (not just contract) caregivers handle payroll taxes, workers’ comp, and liability — protecting your family.
  4. Ask how caregivers are matched. The best McLean agencies match on personality, language, and care needs — not just availability.
  5. Check backup coverage. What happens if a caregiver calls out? Top agencies guarantee replacement coverage.
  6. Request a free in-home assessment. Reputable providers create a written, personalized care plan before service begins.
  7. Read recent reviews. Look for patterns across Google, Caring.com, and word of mouth from Inova Fairfax discharge planners or local geriatric care managers.

FAQs: Home Care in McLean, VA

What is the difference between home care and home health care?

Home care is non-medical (companionship, personal care, housekeeping) and typically paid privately. Home health care is medical (nursing, therapy) prescribed by a doctor and often covered by Medicare after a qualifying event.

How quickly can care start?

Most McLean agencies can begin care within 24–72 hours of the initial assessment; some offer same-day urgent starts after hospital discharge.

Is home care cheaper than assisted living in Fairfax County?

For part-time needs (under ~40 hours/week), home care is usually more affordable. For 24/7 coverage, costs can exceed assisted living, so compare side by side.

Does Medicare pay for home care in Virginia?

Medicare covers skilled home health (nursing/therapy) when medically necessary, but generally does not pay for ongoing non-medical personal care.
